# Cover prompts for "Nico the Little Reindeer's Snowy Day"

Use the SAME ChatGPT chat as the pictures, so Nico and Tilly look the same. Ask for square (1:1),
highest resolution. Send Claude the finished front and back; Claude builds the full wrap cover
(back + spine + front) at KDP's exact size.

## Front cover
```
Now create the FRONT cover of the same book, in full color. Square 1:1, highest resolution.
Same cute kawaii cartoon style with thick clean outlines and soft pastel colors (icy blue, snow white, cherry red, warm honey yellow, mint).
Nico the chubby baby reindeer (round head, tiny branched antlers, big round shiny eyes, tiny smile, round tummy, cozy red scarf) and Tilly the little round penguin (knitted hat with a pom-pom) sitting together on a sled in a snowy meadow, a smiling snowman, snowy pine trees, gently falling snowflakes, a few twinkling stars, a small cottage with a glowing window far behind.
Big bold rounded playful title text at the top: "Nico the Little Reindeer's Snowy Day".
Smaller text under the title: "Color & Read Story Book".
A round badge in the top right corner that says "Ages 3-6".
Keep all text well inside the picture, at least 8% away from every edge. No other text, no watermark.
```

## Back cover
```
Now create the BACK cover of the same book. It must match the front cover exactly: same full-color cute kawaii style, same pastel colors, same Nico and Tilly.
Square 1:1, highest resolution.
- Soft pale icy-blue background fading to a snowy hill at the bottom, a few puffy clouds, small snowflakes and pine trees around the edges
- Small Nico and Tilly waving goodbye in the BOTTOM LEFT corner
- Keep the whole TOP TWO-THIRDS as a calm, plain, light area, because Claude will put the book description text there
- Keep the BOTTOM RIGHT corner completely plain and empty (about one quarter of the width and one eighth of the height) for the barcode
- NO text, NO letters, NO title, NO numbers anywhere on the image
```

If the front cover misspells the title, reply: `The title is wrong. The exact title is: Nico the Little Reindeer's Snowy Day. Redo the same picture with that exact spelling.`
