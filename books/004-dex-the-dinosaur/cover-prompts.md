# Cover prompts for "Dex the Little Dinosaur’s Big Day"

Use the SAME ChatGPT chat as the pictures, so Dex, Pip and Rory look the same. Ask for PORTRAIT (2:3), highest resolution. Send Claude the finished front and back; Claude builds the full wrap cover.

## Front cover
```
Now create the FRONT cover of the same book, in full color. PORTRAIT format (tall, 2:3), highest resolution.
Same cute kawaii cartoon style with thick clean outlines and soft bright colors (leaf green, sky blue, sunny yellow, orange, a touch of purple).
Dex the chubby baby triceratops (round head, three tiny horns, small frill, big round shiny eyes, tiny smile, round tummy) waving happily in the middle, with Pip the baby pterodactyl flying beside him and Rory the tiny baby T-rex peeking out from behind a giant fern. A jungle with giant ferns and palm trees, a friendly round volcano far behind, a big sun, a few puffy clouds.
Big bold rounded playful title text at the top: "Dex the Little Dinosaur's Big Day".
Smaller text under the title: "Color & Read Story Book".
A round badge in the top right corner that says "Ages 3-6".
Keep all text and important details well inside the picture, at least 10% away from every edge. No other text, no watermark.
```

## Back cover
```
Now create the BACK cover of the same book. It must match the front cover exactly: same full-color cute kawaii style, same colors, same Dex, Pip and Rory.
PORTRAIT format (tall, 2:3), highest resolution.
- Soft pale sky-blue and light green background, a few puffy clouds, giant ferns and palm trees around the edges, a small friendly volcano far away
- Small Dex, Pip and Rory waving goodbye in the BOTTOM LEFT corner
- Keep the whole TOP TWO-THIRDS as a calm, plain, light area, because Claude will put the book description text there
- Keep the BOTTOM RIGHT corner completely plain and empty (about one quarter of the width and one eighth of the height) for the barcode
- NO text, NO letters, NO title, NO numbers anywhere on the image
```

If the front cover misspells the title, reply: `The title is wrong. The exact title is: Dex the Little Dinosaur's Big Day. Redo the same picture with that exact spelling.`
