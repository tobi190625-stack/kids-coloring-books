# Scaling organic ads (free reach) without burning out

## Where we are
4 live books × 5 platforms. Accounts are a few days old, so reach is small. Organic growth is slow for the first
4-8 weeks, then compounds if we post every day and repeat the winners. The goal for October: **find 2-3 formats that
reliably bring link clicks.** Then scale those.

## 10 levers, best first
1. **Consistency over volume.** Same times every day. 2 videos/day on TikTok + Shorts beats 6 one day and 0 the next.
2. **Series, not single videos.** "Bedtime story part 1/2/3", "Color with me: page 1 of 29", "Guess the colors #12".
   Series make people follow. Every book can become a series of 10-29 episodes (one per page).
3. **Repurpose everything.** One colored page → coloring video (TikTok/Reels/Shorts) + video pin + image pin +
   carousel slide + read-along clip. Same work, 5-8 posts.
4. **Pinterest is the long game.** Pins keep bringing clicks for months. Search-style titles
   ("Easy Christmas Coloring Pages for Kids 3-6"). 1 pin a day for every book, all year.
5. **Seasonal calendar.** Christmas (Nico) Oct-Dec 10 · Valentine's · Easter (Benny, Posy) · summer · back to school ·
   birthdays all year (Rocco, Posy). Make the next season's ads 6 weeks early.
6. **Free printable page (lead magnet).** "Free coloring page: Nico's snow angel" → people download it from the bio
   → they meet the book. Strong for Pinterest and Facebook.
7. **Parent communities (careful).** Facebook groups for preschool, homeschool, toddler moms. Give value first
   (free page), follow group rules, never spam links.
8. **Collaborations.** Send free copies to small "mom" / homeschool / kids' activity creators (1k-20k followers) and ask
   for an honest video. Disclose it as a gift. One good creator video can beat a month of our own posts.
9. **YouTube long-form for kids' bedtime.** 5-10 min "read-along bedtime story" videos (all pages, calm voice) are
   searched by parents. The Shorts feed the long videos, and the long videos feed the books.
10. **More books = more ads.** Every new book gets a launch week (1 video a day) and joins the rotation. Group books
    into themes (Christmas, animals, vehicles, unicorns/fantasy) so one viewer finds 2-3 books.

## What NOT to do to "scale"
- Don't open 10 accounts per platform or post the same video many times (spam rules, shadow bans).
- Don't pay for ads before a format wins organically and the books have reviews.
- Don't chase views from adults who don't buy kids' books (e.g. ASMR-only audiences). Track link clicks.

## Weekly rhythm at scale (about 5 hours a week for the owner)
- **Sunday (1.5 h):** dashboard check → Claude writes the next week → Claude renders all videos → owner schedules TikTok/YouTube/Meta/Pinterest in one sitting.
- **Daily (20-30 min):** Stories with link, reply to every comment in the first hour, pin a question comment.
- **Mid-week (30 min):** swap in a variation of any early winner.
