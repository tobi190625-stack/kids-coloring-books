How Nico builds a snowman (4 steps)

How do you build the perfect snowman? Nico the little reindeer shows you, in four easy steps.
Step one. Roll a big, round snowball.
Step two. Stack the snowballs, one on top of the other.
Step three. A snowman needs a nose. A carrot!
And step four. Give him a cozy red scarf. Ta-da!
