# Master marketing plan: Benny, Nico, Dex

Goal: get people from social media to the Amazon pages. Amazon does the selling, printing and shipping.
Rule of the game: free organic posting first (months 1-2), small paid tests only after the first sales and reviews.

## Step 0: Before any platform (1 evening)
1. Pick ONE brand name (your pen name or a name like "Cozy Color Stories"). Use the same name and picture on every platform.
2. Whoever owns the KDP account (the adult friend) should own anything that handles money: the Amazon Author Central page, Meta ad billing, Pinterest/TikTok ad billing. You can run the posting.
3. Amazon Author Central (author.amazon.com): log in with the KDP account, claim the 3 books, write a short bio, add a picture. This gives you ONE link that shows all 3 books (the author page). Use it in every bio. Also copy each book's own Amazon link.
4. Make a free folder on your phone called "Books" with: the carousel slides, the pins, the ad squares (they are in the repo under `marketing/<book>/`).
5. A link tool is optional: Linktree or Beacons (free) can hold the author page + each book + a free sample coloring page.

## Step 1: Pinterest (slow, lasts for months)
Setup (15 min):
1. Create a **Business** account (free) at business.pinterest.com. Name = your brand. Add a profile picture (a cover).
2. Create 5 boards: Dinosaur Activities, Christmas and Winter Activities, Cozy Animal Stories, Screen-Free Fun, Gifts for Kids. Fill the board descriptions with keywords (coloring book for kids, toddler activities...).
3. Create pins: upload an image from `marketing/<book>/images/`, title and description from the copy file, **destination link = the book's Amazon link**, pick the matching board.
Routine: 1 pin a day (re-use the 3 designs per book with different titles). Pinterest results take 2-3 months, so don't judge it earlier.
Paid (later): Pinterest ads from $5/day after the book has reviews.

## Step 2: Meta (Facebook + Instagram + Threads)
Setup (30 min):
1. Facebook **Page** (not your personal profile): name = brand, category "Author" or "Book". Add the cover picture.
2. Instagram: switch to a **Professional** account (Settings > Account type), category "Author". Connect it to the Facebook Page.
3. Threads: sign in with the Instagram account.
4. Download Meta Business Suite (free app). Connect the Page + Instagram. It posts to both at once and schedules a week ahead.
5. Bio link = the Amazon author page. Bio text: "Color-and-read story books for ages 3-6. Big letters, big pictures."
Organic posts:
- Carousel (6 slides, from `marketing/<book>/carousel/`): 1 per book per week.
- Single image (the square ad or a pin): 1 per book per week.
- Instagram Stories: share every post to your story with a "Link" sticker to Amazon.
- Threads: a short text post + the first slide, same days.
- Facebook: put the Amazon link in the post (Facebook allows clickable links; Instagram captions do not).
Ads (after the first reviews, about week 3): in Ads Manager choose objective **Traffic**, destination = website, paste the Amazon link, format **Carousel** (use the same 6 slides) or single image. $5-$10/day for 5 days, audience US, ages 25-65, interests: coloring books, preschool, homeschooling, dinosaurs / Christmas gifts (matching the book). Turn off any ad that costs more than about $0.80 per click or has clicks but no sales after $30. Ads need an adult payer and Meta may ask to confirm the identity of the Page owner.
AI label: if Meta asks whether the images are AI-made, answer honestly (Yes).

## Step 3: TikTok (organic)
Setup (15 min): create an account with the brand name, switch to a **Business account** (Settings > Account) so the link in bio works. Bio link = Amazon author page.
Content (3-5 posts a week; the ideas are in each book's plan file):
- **Photo carousels:** upload the 6 carousel slides as a TikTok photo post with a trending sound. Zero editing.
- **Videos** (CapCut, free): page-flip through the book, a timelapse of coloring one page, before/after, "gift idea" with crayons and a bow.
- Caption: the hook line + 3-5 hashtags (#coloringbook #kidsactivities #screenfree #toddleractivities #dinosaurs / #christmasgifts).
- Only show real kids with a parent's permission. Hands only is fine.
TikTok Shop: skip (the seller has to ship; KDP books can't be fulfilled that way).

## Step 4: More ideas that fit this business
1. **YouTube Shorts:** upload the same TikTok videos as Shorts. Free extra reach. Channel link = Amazon author page.
2. **Facebook groups** for parents, preschool teachers and homeschoolers: read the rules, give value first (a free coloring page), mention the book only where allowed. Never spam.
3. **Free sample page:** make a free 3-page PDF (I can make it) and offer it on Pinterest and Instagram. It builds trust and a follower list.
4. **Reviews:** after the books are live, give a few copies to family and friends and ask them for honest reviews. Never buy reviews. Reviews matter most for sales.
5. **Amazon Ads** (best paid channel): 2-3 weeks after launch, Sponsored Products, Auto campaign, $5/day per book. Pause anything with 20+ clicks and no sales.
6. **Seasonal pushes:** Nico from October to mid-December; Dex around birthdays, summer and Christmas; Benny at Easter and spring.
7. **Translations** (German, Spanish): later, one language per book per country store.

## 4-week calendar
Week 1: Step 0 and set up all accounts. Post each book's first carousel. 1 pin a day.
Week 2: 3 carousels + 3 single images + 4 TikToks. Ask friends for reviews.
Week 3: Same rhythm. Start Meta ad test for the book with the best reviews/sales rank ($5/day).
Week 4: Review what worked (see below). Double down on that format. Start Amazon Ads.

## Weekly review (every Sunday, 15 min)
Look at KDP Reports: units sold per book. Look at which post or pin got the most saves/clicks and make more like it. Stop what gets nothing after 3 tries.

## Rules and safety
- Don't promise income or health claims. Don't write fake reviews.
- Don't use real children's faces without a parent's permission.
- Answer AI-label questions honestly.
- The adult account holder reports and pays tax on the royalties (see the tax notes).
