# 01 – Meta Ad Library research: kids' coloring / activity / color-and-read books

Researched: 2026-10-04. Source: Meta Ad Library (facebook.com/ads/library), read-only and not logged in. The library showed in Czech because of location. Filters were "All ads" and "Active", sorted by **total impressions (most first)**. Countries: United States, ALL and Germany (DE). Germany is in the EU, so Meta shows real **reach numbers** there.
Ad links use this format: `https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=<ID>`

---

## 1. TL;DR (5 most important findings)

1. **Almost nobody runs paid Meta ads for "color & read" story books.** "color and read" returned 0 relevant ads, "truck coloring" ~3 results, and Bobbie Goods (the #1 seller) runs basically no ads because they grew organically. There is no ad competition for our niche, but there is also no proven ad to copy, so we must test.
2. **Small KDP authors DO run ads to Amazon, and they last.** Examples: a German toddler coloring book image ad, 4 versions, **213,424 reach**, running 4+ months; Lillian Fredrick Publishing's 10-card carousel to Amazon; Big Happy Books' "Read, Color, Build!" carousel, running **~1 year**. The simple format works: book image, "Simple, big pictures", "ages 1-3", Shop Now.
3. **The biggest spenders (toddla ~1,100 ads, Nuesthetics, Tibatoes, Neefty, Cupkin) sell "screen-free quiet time" to tired parents, not "coloring".** The repeating hooks are *the tablet problem*, *minutes of silence* and *no mess*.
4. **Video wins for DTC brands and carousel/image wins for book authors.** Video is used for "watch it happen" product demos. Book publishers use **carousels of covers + inside pages** with Amazon links and keep them running for months.
5. **Season + gift angle is when book ads appear.** Halloween, Christmas and "gift that isn't more candy" ads all start in Sept/early Oct. Christmas coloring ads were launched mostly 17–30 Sept 2026. Nico (reindeer) needs ads live **from late October**.

## 3. Winning formats (ranked)

1. **Short "watch it happen" video (vertical Reel), with no people or just hands.** Evidence: toddla (~1,100 ads), Nuesthetics (6+ months), Neefty (22 months), Cupkin (5 months), plus every coloring app (dozens of video variants). For us, a **coloring timelapse** is the same idea: a page "magically" fills with color.
2. **Carousel of book covers / inside pages → Amazon or shop.** Evidence: Big Happy Books (~1 year, 4+ carousel ads with 5–8 cards), Lillian Fredrick (10 cards, Amazon). Each card has its own headline: age + "Simple, Big Pictures" + Shop Now. Best for **showing the whole series** (Benny + Nico + coming soon Rocco/Posy).
3. **Single image of the book + benefit text → Amazon.** Evidence: Bernd Raßtal DE: 213k reach, 4 versions, 4 months. Graffiti book 36k. Color Fluff. Cheap and simple, and it works for KDP.
4. **Boosted Instagram post/Reel → profile ("link in bio").** Evidence: merri_meadow (KDP, 2 variants this week), peelmarkstudio, YN Book Creator (17 months). Builds followers + Amazon clicks. Good for a teen with a small budget.
5. **Free printable lead magnet (free coloring pages → email/Instagram).** Evidence: iBelieve, Crosswalk, Universal, YN Book Creator (17 months), CraftNest (6 copies). This gets cheap clicks, but it's a funnel (needs email or follow), not a direct sale.

## 4. Hooks & copy patterns that repeat

**A. Screen-time / tablet pain (most used by the big spenders)**
- "You hand over the tablet just to survive dinner" (toddla)
- "He asks for his painting book instead of the tablet now." (toddla, review quote)
- "No screens. No mess." (Digital Planner Sheets)
- "Most kids do not need another screen." (Christianity Cove)
- "screen-free" appears in many hashtags (#screenfreeactivities, #ScreenFreeFun)

**B. Quiet time / parent relief (humor)**
- "10 minutes of silence… I thought something was wrong 😂" (TryKorlem)
- "buys you 40 minutes of quiet" (toddla)
- "Keep the kids busy for HOURS" (YN Book Creator)

**C. Name the child's obsession (question)**
- "Does your toddler stop everything when they see a bulldozer…?" (Big Happy Books)
- "Little animal lover?" (Big Happy Books)
- "Looking for a magical screen free activity for kids? 🦄" (Unicorn Wonder Days)

**D. Social proof**
- "Parents are raving about…" (Nuesthetics)
- "⭐ Rated 4.8 of 5 Stars" / "4.9/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐" / "100,000+ Happy Parents"

**E. Use-case / moment**
- "Ideal for restaurant waits or trips" (Funlearnkids)
- "Need an awesome travel or home activity…" (Cupkin)
- "Tired of hearing 'I'm done! Now what?'" (Teaching Second Grade)

**F. Gift / season**
- "a cute Halloween gift that isn't more candy?" (peelmarkstudio)
- "Great for Boo Baskets" / "Stocking Stuffer" (listing titles)
- "Seasonal books for little ones! 🎃🍂🦃🎄" (Lillian Fredrick)

**G. Pattern-break / funny**
- "GOATWHAAAT?! 🐐 When AI makes a kids' coloring book… and skips the editor 😂" (merri_meadow). It mocks bad AI books and then says theirs is "human-checked".

**H. Scarcity / restock**
- "It's back! 🎉 …restocked" (Neefty, 22 months running)
- "We owe you an apology…" (Tibatoes)

**Copy structure used by most ads:** 1-line hook → 3–5 emoji bullet benefits (✨🎨📖) → age range → CTA. Headlines are usually **the book title + age**, for example "My First Autumn Coloring Book for Toddlers 1-3".

## 5. Visual style patterns

- **No real kids needed in book ads.** Book publishers (Big Happy Books, Lillian Fredrick, Bernd Raßtal, Color Fluff) show **covers + inside pages** only. DTC toy brands use kids/hands, but we don't have to.
- **Timelapse coloring with no people** is standard in app ads ("Relax With Christmas Coloring", "Zen Color"): a black-and-white line art page fills with color.
- **Bright, saturated colors** on the colored version vs. the clean black-and-white page = built-in "before → after".
- **Big text on screen** in the first second (a question or "POV"). Emojis in the copy. Lots of 🎨🖍️✨🦄🚜🎄.
- **Character first:** peelmark (ghost + cats), merri_meadow (goat). Ads feature one cute character, which fits Benny/Nico/Rocco/Posy.
- **Length:** most video ads show "0:00 / 0:00" in the library (we could not read the duration). Product-demo style suggests about 10–30 s Reels.
- **Seasonal décor** (pumpkins, snow, Christmas) in Sept–Dec creatives.
- **Carousel cards:** one book per card, a clean background, a short headline per card.

## 6. Offers / CTAs / landing pages

- **CTA buttons:** "Shop Now" is by far the most common. Then "Learn More", "Download" (lead magnets), "Visit Instagram profile" (boosted posts), "Order Now", "Sign Up", "Send WhatsApp message".
- **Landing pages:**
  - **Amazon direct** (Lillian Fredrick, Cupkin, Color Fluff, Unicorn Wonder Days, Bernd Raßtal, Graffiti) is common for KDP. Meta can't track Amazon sales, so use **Amazon Attribution links** (free in Amazon Ads console) to see which ad sells ([F. Ferguson Books](https://www.ffergusonbooks.com/blog/blog-post-title-two-8fspj)).
  - **Own Shopify shop** (toddla, Nuesthetics, Big Happy Books, Tibatoes, Neefty) is used by brands with bundles and discounts.
  - **Instagram profile** (merri_meadow, peelmark, YN Book Creator) is used for "link in bio" with cheap boosts.
  - **Gumroad PDF $4.99** / free printable → email (Digital Planner Sheets, iBelieve, Crosswalk).
- **Offers:** % off (40–60%), "Get 1 Free", bundles ("Get all three for less"), free printable, restock/limited. KDP authors usually have **no discount**. They just show the price and the age.

## 7. What Little Crayon Tales should copy (5 ad concepts, no kids filmed)

All 9:16 Reels for IG/FB (also post organically on TikTok/Shorts). End card: cover + "Little Crayon Tales – on Amazon" + age 3-6. Use an **Amazon Attribution link** for each concept.

**Concept 1 – "Watch Benny come to life" (coloring timelapse)**
- Format: vertical video, 12–15 s. A black-and-white page fills with crayon color, then cut to the cover.
- Hook text (on screen, 1st second): "Color the page. Read the sentence. 🖍️📖"
- Voice/text 2: "One big picture + one easy sentence on every page."
- Caption: "A cozy color-and-read story for ages 3-6 🐻 Big pictures, big letters, no tiny details. Perfect for quiet time & bedtime. Link in bio / Shop Now."
- CTA: Shop Now (Amazon) or boost → profile.

**Concept 2 – "Screen-free quiet time" (pain hook, copy toddla/TryKorlem)**
- Format: text-on-screen slideshow + timelapse, 15–20 s. Background voice from TTS (no kid).
- Hook: "POV: you hid the tablet and it was… quiet? 😂"
- Slide 2: "10 cozy minutes. One page. One story sentence."
- Slide 3: flip-through of 4 pages. Slide 4: cover + "ages 3-6".
- Caption: "Swap 10 minutes of screen time for coloring + reading 🖍️ Benny the Bear's Cozy Day is a bold & easy color-and-read book for ages 3-6. #screenfreeactivities #toddleractivities"

**Concept 3 – Series carousel (copy Big Happy Books / Lillian Fredrick)**
- Format: carousel, 4–6 cards, 1:1 or 4:5.
- Card 1: Benny cover + "Cozy everyday story – ages 3-6". Card 2: Benny page uncolored vs colored ("Big, easy shapes"). Card 3: Nico cover + "Christmas & snow story". Card 4: Nico page before/after. Card 5 (later): Rocco "Coming soon – for truck lovers 🚚".
- Primary text: "Color it. Read it. Love it. 🖍️📖 Little Crayon Tales are story books where every page has ONE big picture and ONE easy sentence, made for ages 3-6."
- Headline per card: book title + age. CTA: Shop Now → Amazon.

**Concept 4 – "Little reindeer fan?" Christmas gift (season + identity question)**
- Format: video 15 s (snowy page colored in) OR single image with a gift/stocking mockup made digitally.
- Hook: "Little reindeer fan? 🦌❄️" → "A Christmas story they color AND read."
- Text: "Stocking stuffer that isn't more candy 🎄" (copied from the peelmark idea).
- Caption: "Nico the Little Reindeer's Snowy Day 🦌 29 cozy winter pages with big, easy words, ages 3-6. A perfect stocking stuffer & Christmas Eve box idea. Shop Now."
- Run **late Oct → Dec 20**. Christmas ads start in late Sept/Oct, so don't start too late.

**Concept 5 – Pattern-break "made by a human, not a robot" (copy merri_meadow, but positive)**
- Format: video 10–12 s. Show one "bad" messy AI-style line art (drawn by us as a joke) → swipe to our clean bold page → timelapse color.
- Hook: "Why do some kids' coloring books look… weird? 🤨"
- Text 2: "Ours: thick lines, big shapes, one easy sentence per page."
- Caption: "Every page checked by hand ✅ Made for little hands (ages 3-6). Little Crayon Tales on Amazon." (Only say "human-checked" if it is true. Never make fake claims.)
- Bonus for boys/girls later: "Does your kid stop everything for a monster truck? 🚚" (Rocco) / "Little unicorn lover? 🦄" (Posy).

**Extra (cheap traffic test):** a free 3-page "Benny printable" lead magnet (like iBelieve/YN Book Creator) on Pinterest/IG, with "Want the whole story? Full book on Amazon" on the last page.

**Test plan:** first boost the best organic Reel (€/$3–5 a day, 3–5 days). Then try Concept 1 vs 2 vs 3 with the same budget. Target women 25-45 / parents of preschoolers (the German KDP ad that is still running targets **women 18-65+**). Measure link clicks + Amazon Attribution sales.

## 8. What to avoid

- **Fake claims** ("100,000 happy parents", fake reviews, fake "sold out/restock"). The DTC brands do it, but we are a small honest brand, and Amazon/Meta can punish fake claims.
- **Discount pressure we can't control.** We can't change the Amazon price easily. Don't promise "60% off".
- **Showing kids' faces or hands from stock/AI** that look fake. Product-only creatives work fine for book ads.
- **Too much text / tiny details** in the image. Book ads that work have one cover, one benefit and one age.
- **Generic "coloring book for kids" positioning.** That space is full of apps, adult books and big brands. Lead with **"color AND read story"**, the character, and the age, because no one else does.
- **Sending paid traffic to Amazon without Amazon Attribution.** Then you can't know which ad sold books.
- **Starting Christmas ads in December.** Competitors launch in Sept/Oct. Late ads miss shipping windows.
- **"Comment X for link" automation bait** unless we can really answer DMs fast (it's an influencer tactic).

## 9. Confidence & gaps

- **Confidence: medium.** Ad texts, start dates, CTA, landing domains and version counts were read directly from the library. EU reach numbers (DE) were read from ad detail pages.
- **Gaps:**
  - The browser tab was hidden, so **infinite scroll did not load**. I saw only the top ~30 ads (by impressions) per search. There are likely more small KDP ads deeper in the results.
  - I could not **see the videos** (no screenshots), so video length, the first frame and on-screen text are inferred from copy and format, not watched.
  - **US ads show no reach/spend** (Meta only shows that for EU and political ads). US "winners" are judged by longevity + number of variants only.
  - Searches for "Bobbie Goods", "truck coloring" and "color and read" returned almost nothing. This is likely real (few ads), but the library search was flaky at times.
  - Reach was checked for only 5 DE ads (time limit). No CZ ads checked.
  - No data on CTR/CPC. Meta does not publish it.
