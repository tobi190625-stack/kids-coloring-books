# 02 - Meta Ad Library: competitor deep dive (kids coloring / activity books)

Research date: 4 Oct 2026. Source: Meta Ad Library (facebook.com/ads/library), country = US, **active ads only**.
Method: keyword searches ("kids coloring book", "toddler coloring book", "activity book for kids", "sticker book kids", "christmas coloring book", "bold and easy coloring", "personalized coloring book", brand names, and more). Then I opened each advertiser's own page in the Ad Library and read its 30 top ads (sorted by impressions), formats, landing pages and start dates. I used a start-date filter to find the oldest ads that are still running.
"cc" = how many copies of the same creative are running (more copies = they scale it).

---

## 1. TL;DR

1. **Short vertical VIDEO is the main format.** Cupkin 17/17 video, Melissa & Doug Water WOW 22/22 video, toddla 20/30, Usborne 5/5. Still images and carousels are used most for **gift / personalized** books and for small publishers.
2. **The #1 hook is "screen-free / no tablet / quiet time for the parent".** It is in Cupkin, Melissa & Doug, toddla, Highlights and My Colorful World ads. The second hook is **"gift"** (grandma, Christmas, "the gift they will actually remember").
3. **Amazon is a normal landing page.** Cupkin (17/17 to Amazon), Usborne (5/5 Amazon), Melissa & Doug (Amazon is its biggest retailer link for Water WOW), Little Hippo, Pick-A-Page ("shipped fast via Amazon"). So it is fine for us to send ads straight to our Amazon listing.
4. **The ads that last longest are simple and evergreen.** Big Happy Books' 5-card carousel "Read, Color, Build!" has run since 20 Oct 2025 (almost 1 year). Pick-A-Page's "personalized gift" ads have run since 10 Nov 2025. Little Hippo's sensory-book video has run since 3 Sep 2025.
5. **Q4 starts NOW.** Wonderbly went fully Christmas on 16 Sep, Usborne has an Advent "24 Books to Unwrap Before Christmas" ad since 15 Sep, Dylanna Publishing started 17 new ads on 1 Oct, and Melissa & Doug started about 15 Water WOW ads on 1-2 Oct (Prime Day). Nico (Christmas) ads should be ready by mid/late October.

## 3. Per-competitor notes (best ads)

### Cupkin (closest model for us: activity book, Amazon landing, video only)
- **"How do they behave so well without a screen? This."** Video, 500 hand-drawn stickers plus coloring scenes, links to Amazon. *Why it works:* it starts with a question about the parent's problem, and the answer is the book. It is 2 sentences long.
- **"Step 1: get the book. Step 2: watch them disappear into it."** Video. *Why:* a funny 2-step pattern, easy to read on mute.
- **"Bad day? Turn into a fairy."** / **"The Halloween activity that actually holds their attention."** Video. *Why:* one theme per ad (fairy, ocean, space, dogs, sports, princess). Each child interest gets its own ad. This is exactly our plan with Benny / Nico / Rocco / Posy.
- Pattern: 4 evergreen ads from May are still live. On 28 Sep they added 13 new ads, mostly seasonal (Halloween) and one per theme.

### Melissa & Doug - Water WOW!
- **"PAW Patrol Water WOW! is Basically Magic"**. Video, 8 copies running. *Why:* it shows the "reveal" moment. Color appears on the page = satisfying to watch. **This is the same visual idea as our coloring timelapse.**
- **"We've all been the parent on the road trip who's only one hour in and already losing their sanity..."** Video. *Why:* a real-life pain plus a relatable parent voice.
- **"Your Screen-Free Morning Hack"**. Video, running since 22 Jun (the oldest M&D ad still live).
- "Kids Coloring Activities ... Add to cart for Prime Day". They launched about 15 ads on 1-2 Oct for the October Prime sale.

### Highlights for Children
- **"100% Screen-Free Fun"**. Dynamic ad.
- **"What can we do today? Solved"**. *Why:* it names the parent's daily question.
- **"Your #1 Grandma Trophy"**. Video. *Why:* it targets grandparents as buyers (gifts).
- **"Must-Have for Kids Ages 2-6: waiting at a restaurant, flying, running errands..."** *Why:* it lists real situations.

### Usborne Publishing
- **"24 Books to Unwrap Before Christmas"**. Video, started 15 Sep, links to Amazon. *Why:* a Christmas ritual idea (Advent). It sells a habit, not only a book.
- **"Spooktacular Books for Halloween"**. Video, 2 copies.

### Big Happy Books (closest to our "read + color" idea)
- **5-card carousel, live since 20 Oct 2025:** "Start Building Together Today!", "Learn With Board Books", "Start Coloring Together Today!", "Color Your Favorites!", "Read, Color, Build!". ## 4. Patterns across winners

**Formats ranked (for kids book/activity products):**
1. **Vertical video, 10-30 s, product being used** (pages turning, color/stickers appearing, the "reveal"). The most used and the most scaled (most copies).
2. **Carousel of pages/products** (Big Happy Books, M&D). Used by small book sellers, and it lasts a long time.
3. **Dynamic catalog / DCO** (Highlights, Wonderbly, Pick-A-Page). Needs a product catalog/store. **Not for us now.**
4. **Static image with gift text** (My Colorful World, Scrively). Cheap to test many hooks.

**Hooks ranked:**
1. **Screen-free / "instead of the tablet" / quiet time for parents** (Cupkin, M&D, toddla, Highlights, My Colorful World).
2. **Gift** (Christmas, grandparents, "they'll actually remember", stocking stuffer).
3. **Real situations**: road trip, restaurant, plane, rainy afternoon, morning routine.
4. **Kid's obsession theme**: trucks, dinos, fairies, princess, PAW Patrol. One theme per ad.
5. **Magic / satisfying reveal**: "basically magic", color appearing.
6. **Social proof quote** as the headline ("He asks for his painting book instead of the tablet now").

**Visuals:** bright colors on screen; the product filling the frame; a close-up of the page being colored or revealed. Many show hands and kids (UGC). We can't film those, so we use a **coloring-timelapse screen recording + page flip-through mockups** instead. The "reveal" effect works the same way.

**Copy length:** the hook is 1 short line (5-10 words). The body is 2-3 sentences. Emojis are common (🎨🦖🚚🎄). Hashtags are used in UGC-style ads (Cupkin, M&D).

**CTA:** "Shop Now" / "Buy" ("Koupit") is the most common. Then "Order Now" for gift/personalized products and "Learn more" for subscriptions.

**Seasonality (Christmas Q4):**
- Halloween creatives went live 12-28 Sep (Usborne, Cupkin).
- Christmas creatives went live from **mid-September** (Wonderbly 16 Sep, Usborne Advent 15 Sep).
- A big refresh happened on **1-2 Oct** (Dylanna 17 new ads, Melissa & Doug about 15 new ads for the October Prime sale).
- Personalized/gift brands started their winning Christmas ads in **early-mid November** (Pick-A-Page 10 Nov 2025), and those ads kept running all year.
- **For us:** have Nico ads ready by about 20 Oct. Push hardest from mid-November to about 15 Dec (Amazon shipping cut-off). Benny (cozy/bedtime) can run all year.

## 5. What Little Crayon Tales should copy (5 ad concepts, no kids or hands on camera)

**Concept 1: "Color appears" timelapse (Benny)**
- Format: 9:16 video, 12-15 s. A screen-recorded digital coloring timelapse of one Benny page (black and white → full color, fast). End on the book cover mockup plus "On Amazon".
- On-screen hook (first second): **"Watch Benny's cozy day come to life 🐻🖍️"**
- Second text: "One big picture. One easy sentence. Every page."
- Caption: "A color & read story book for ages 3-6. Big bold pages your little one can actually finish. 🐻 Benny the Bear's Cozy Day, on Amazon."
- Why: it copies M&D's "basically magic" reveal and the satisfying-video trend.

**Concept 2: "Screen-free quiet time" problem-solution (Benny or Nico)**
- Format: 9:16 slideshow with voiceover (TTS or the owner's voice), 15-20 s. Slide 1: a simple illustration of a tablet with a big ❌. Slides 2-5: real book pages flipping (mockups). Last slide: the cover.
- Hook: **"How do you get 20 quiet minutes without a tablet? 👇"**
- Voiceover: "Give them a story they color themselves. Big bold pictures, one easy sentence per page, made for ages 3 to 6."
- Caption: "Screen-free time that still feels like story time. 🖍️📖"
- Why: it is the #1 hook in the category (Cupkin, toddla, Highlights, M&D).

**Concept 3: Christmas gift carousel (Nico), the Big Happy Books template**
- Format: 5-card carousel (square 1:1), static page mockups.
- Card 1: "Meet Nico the Little Reindeer 🦌", Card 2: "Big bold pages for little hands", Card 3: "One easy sentence per page: color AND read", Card 4: "A cozy winter story to color together", Card 5: "Perfect stocking stuffer 🎄 [Shop Now]".
- Caption hook: **"The Christmas book they'll color again and again."**
- Run: late Oct to mid Dec. Why: carousels like this have run for about 1 year at Big Happy Books, and gift hooks peak in Q4.

**Concept 4: "Kid obsession" hook for Rocco (launch ad)**
- Format: 9:16 video, 10-15 s. Fast flip-through of 5-6 monster truck pages plus 1 timelapse page, with an engine sound effect.
- Hook: **"Does your kid stop everything when a monster truck drives by? 🚚💥"**
- Caption: "Rocco the Monster Truck: a big, bold color & read book for ages 3-6. Vroom on every page."
- Why: it copies Big Happy Books' bulldozer question and Cupkin's one-theme-per-ad method. Do the same for Posy: "Is your little one unicorn-obsessed? 🦄"

**Concept 5: "Real situations" list (works for all books)**
- Format: 9:16 text-on-screen video, 8-12 s, 4 quick scenes made from illustrations or icons: "🍽️ waiting for food", "✈️ on the plane", "🚗 long car ride", "🌙 before bed", then the book cover.
- Hook: **"Restaurant. Plane. Car. Bedtime. One little book. 🖍️"**
- Caption: "Small, cozy, and easy to finish. Color & read story books for ages 3-6."
- Why: it copies Highlights' "waiting at a restaurant, flying..." and M&D's road trip angle. Short, so it works on mute.

Extra test idea (cheap): **"gift quote" static images.** One image of the book cover on a cozy background plus the line "A gift they'll color, read, and keep." Test 3-4 text versions like My Colorful World does.

## 6. What to avoid

- **Fake urgency or price promises** ("50% off ends tonight"). Amazon sets our price, and Meta may flag it.
- **Fake reviews or made-up quotes.** Only use real Amazon review text (with permission and context), never invent "⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ said a mom".
- **Using licensed characters** (PAW Patrol, Disney). M&D can do it because they pay for the license. We can't.
- **Dynamic/catalog ads**. They need a store + catalog. Not useful while we sell only on Amazon.
- **Adult "bold & easy" style messaging** ("relax", "anxiety relief", "your happy place"). That is the adult coloring market. Our buyer is the parent or grandparent of a 3-6 year old.
- **Too many words on screen.** Winners use one hook line plus the product. Don't put the whole book description in the video.
- **Starting Christmas too late.** Competitors had Christmas ads live by mid-September to early October.
- **Pretending to be a big brand.** Don't copy the names, logos or exact ad text of other brands. Copy the *structure* only.

## 7. Confidence & gaps

- **Confidence: medium.** The formats, hooks, landing pages and start dates come straight from the Ad Library (US, active ads, 4 Oct 2026). Active ad counts are Meta's own totals per page.
- **The Ad Library does NOT show clicks, spend or sales** for these commercial ads. "Winner" is guessed from how long an ad has run (longevity) and how many copies are running. That is a signal, not proof.
- I read **the top 30 ads per advertiser** (by impressions), not every ad. For big accounts (Melissa & Doug 436, toddla 571, Wonderbly 244) this is only a sample.
- I could not watch the videos (hidden browser window, no screenshots). Video **length and visual style are inferred** from the text and format, not measured. Next step: open 3-5 of these ads by hand and note the length and the first 3 seconds.
- Not researched because time ran out: Dylanna Publishing's ad texts (only counts and dates), Lay It Flat Publishing, Planning Playtime, Pippi Post, Raising Toddlers (upairy, 82 image ads), Coloring Book Cafe, Funlearnkids. Also non-US countries (UK/DE) and inactive/past Q4 2025 ads (to see last year's full Christmas curve).
- Bobbie Goods / Coco Wyo / Lisa Frank / Kumon / Paper Peony / Crayola main page: no active US Meta ads were found by keyword search. They may advertise under another page name. Low confidence on "zero".
