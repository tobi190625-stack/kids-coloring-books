# 04 - TikTok Commercial Content Library (EU/UK ad library)

Source: https://library.tiktok.com/ads (the DSA ad library). It shows ads that ran in the EU, EEA, Switzerland and UK, with first and last shown dates, unique-user reach, and targeting.
Research date: 4 Oct 2026. Search window: 4 Oct 2025 to 4 Oct 2026 (last 12 months), all countries.
Method: about 45 keyword searches in 9 languages, about 700 ads read, and the detail pages of 13 key ads opened for targeting. Read-only: no login, no clicks on ads.

---

## 1) TL;DR (5 lines)

1. **Kids' coloring books hardly advertise on TikTok in the EU.** After about 45 searches I found only around 15 real kids' book or activity book advertisers, and almost all of them reached **0 to 10K people**. The space is close to empty, so it is cheap to stand out, but there are no proven "big winners" to copy.
2. **The longest-running ad is a tiny KDP-style ad.** "Zauberhaftes Feen-Malbuch! 50 magische Bilder ... Jetzt auf Amazon!" (online360, Germany and Austria) ran for **583 days** (Feb 2025 to Oct 2026). It is a simple flip-through/coloring video with a **Traffic objective** that sends people to Amazon. Our model is the same: cheap, simple, and always on.
3. **The ads with the most reach use a clear "benefit + who it's for" hook**, for example the PageEight bilingual coloring books (10K-100K in 5 days), personalised books ("Name and photo of your child in every scene"), and Macmillan's "Get cosy this autumn ... sticker book" (cosy and seasonal, like Benny and Nico).
4. **Small sellers mostly "Promote" their own organic posts** (creator handles with hashtags, like gcreativegroup, lienertsbuecherwelt, pw.books). In practice these are Spark-style ads. Outside research says Spark Ads get about 25% higher CTR, about 24-43% higher conversion rate and about 142% more engagement than normal ads. **So the plan is: post organically first, then boost the post that wins.**
5. **Targeting is broad.** Kids' book ads in the EU/UK target **all adults 18+, all genders**, often with no interests at all (the algorithm finds parents through the creative). The only interest used was "Baby & Kids Products" (HYPEZ). By TikTok policy you must target 18+ and speak to parents, not to kids.

## 3) Winning formats ranked (with evidence)

Ranking = longevity first (an advertiser keeps paying only for what works), then reach. Kids' coloring book ads have little reach data, so I also used kids' coloring apps (same audience, same visuals).

| Rank | Format | Evidence from the library | Can we make it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Simple product video + "on Amazon" + Traffic objective, always on with a small budget** | online360 fairy Malbuch: **583 days**. Hachette: 185 days of Traffic ads. | YES: a flip-through + coloring timelapse with the Amazon cover at the end |
| 2 | **"Satisfying / ASMR" coloring-fill video** | GAME DISTRICT "Coloring ASMR for Kids" 55 d, 60+ variants; MWM 62 d, 31 variants; Princess app 52 d | YES: our coloring timelapse is exactly this |
| 3 | **Benefit / learning hook ("learn words while coloring")** | PageEight bilingual coloring books: **10K-100K in 5 days**, the top coloring book reach; Librio 22 variants; Tweezorg Montessori | YES: "color AND read", "first words", "big letters" |
| 4 | **Cosy / seasonal mood (autumn, Christmas)** | Macmillan "Get cosy this autumn" 7K reach, 20 d; gcreativegroup "#christmaspresents" in early Oct; Usborne testing 12 creatives from late Sep | YES: Benny = cosy autumn and bedtime, Nico = Christmas |
| 5 | **Boosted organic post from the creator account (Spark / Promote)** | lienertsbuecherwelt, gcreativegroup, pw.books, jusy758, ceeceeharajukubarbie all paid to promote their own posts | YES: the cheapest test. Boost only posts that already do well organically |
| 6 | **Image / carousel ad** | Macmillan (2 image ads, 7K), Bolly's Busy Books (image, 3K, 17 d), Micro Scooters (11 image ads, 52 d) | YES: photo carousel of pages |
| 7 | **Personalised / "your child is the hero"** | HYPEZ, Librio, THINK ABOUT | Not now (our books are not personalised), but we can borrow the "your child" words |
| Loser | **Book title only, many variants, no hook** | I Can Color Too!: 16 variants, stopped after 2 days. Bidaya "order now": 5 days | Avoid |

Outside evidence (web):
- Spark Ads vs normal ads: TikTok's own data says **+25% CTR and +24% conversion rate**. Agency data says +30% completion rate, +142% engagement, +43% CVR and about 4% lower CPM. ([joinstatus](https://brands.joinstatus.com/tiktok-spark-ads-vs-non-spark-ads), [dynamoi](https://dynamoi.com/learn/tiktok-music-promotion/tiktok-spark-ads-vs-non-spark-ads), [tikadsuite](https://tikadsuite.com/blog/spark-ads-vs-non-spark-ads/))
- Photo carousels on TikTok (study of about 700K posts, Jan-May 2025): **+81% engagement rate**, about the same reach (+3%), but **about 33% fewer shares** than videos. Carousels are good for "how-to / what's inside" content. ([Fanpage Karma](https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/carousel-vs-video-performance-tiktok-instagram/))
- TikTok creative rules: hook in the first 3 seconds (recall) and keep it strong up to 6 seconds (engagement); 9:16; sound on; 5-10 words per second of on-screen text max; "not overly polished"; test 3-5 creatives per ad group. ([TikTok Ads Help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices))
- Book ads on TikTok: CPC about $0.40-1.20 when it works, Amazon conversion about 3-8% of clicks. Single books tend to only break even, and a **series** makes the money. ([Vuga Publishing](https://vugapublishing.com/blog/46-tiktok-ads-for-books-roi)) Little Crayon Tales is a series, which helps.

## 4) Hooks and text patterns (short exact quotes)

- **Number + theme + where to buy:** "50 magische Bilder zum Ausmalen ... Jetzt auf Amazon!" (online360)
- **Question about a parent's goal:** "Raising bilingual kids?" (Librio), "Gemakkelijk Papiamentu leren?" (PageEight)
- **Parent pain + solution:** "The gift kids actually finish" (Trendflow)
- **Cosy / season:** "Get cosy this autumn with the adorable ..." (Macmillan)
- **Child as hero:** "A bedtime story starring your child" (THINK ABOUT), "Name und Foto deines Kindes in jeder Szene" (HYPEZ)
- **Contrast with boring options:** "keine Vorlagen von der Stange" (= "no off-the-shelf templates")
- **TikTok-native phrases:** "this is your sign" (MWM), "POV:" (DickObraz)
- **Satisfying words:** "Coloring ASMR for Kids", "Satisfying Coloring" (GAME DISTRICT)
- **Mood line for adults:** "Stifte raus, Alltag aus!" (= "Pens out, everyday life off!") (Lienerts)
- **Gift + season hashtags:** "#christmaspresents" (gcreativegroup, early October)

Patterns that did NOT last: hashtag-only captions (#kinderbuch #autor), the book title alone, and "Order now!" with no reason.

**Ready-to-use hook templates for us:**
- "Your 3-year-old can't read yet? This book fixes that while they color." (question + benefit)
- "The coloring book kids actually finish: one big picture, one short sentence." (pain + solution)
- "This is your sign to swap screen time for a cozy bedtime coloring story." (native phrase)
- "POV: bedtime takes 10 minutes because of this bear 🐻" (POV)
- "27 big, bold pages. One cozy story. On Amazon." (number + where)

## 5) Targeting insights (who sees kids' book ads)

- **Age:** in the UK ads I opened (Bolly's Busy Books, Trendflow, gcreativegroup) the targeting was **all 18-24 through 55+**, with 13-17 excluded. Macmillan's Moomin sticker book also included 13-17 (a brand IP for a teen/adult cosy audience). EU ads hide the age split ("-"), so they are probably broad as well.
- **Gender:** every ad I opened targeted **male + female + unknown**. Nobody narrowed to "women only", even though the buyers are mostly moms. The algorithm does the narrowing.
- **Interests:** almost always **none**. The one exception was HYPEZ with "Baby & Kids Products". Hachette used "Education" for an education product. Librio used an **exclusion audience** (it hides ads from past buyers or visitors).
- **Location + language:** ads are per language market (DE+AT+CH German, FR, NL, CZ, UK). The online360 ad added "high spending power = yes".
- **Objective:** small book sellers use **Traffic** (clicks to Amazon), and some use Sales or Reach. Traffic is the natural choice for Amazon KDP because we cannot put a TikTok pixel on Amazon.
- **Who uses TikTok:** 25-34 is the biggest age group (about 40% of the ad audience), and women are about 44% of the ad audience. The "Baby, Kids & Maternity" interest audience is about 624M people. ([DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/essential-tiktok-stats), [tikadtools targeting](https://tikadtools.com/blog/tiktok-ads-targeting/))
- **Policy:** in the US, UK and EU, ads for products that appeal to children under 13 must **target adults 18+ only** and must not use a "childish" creative that talks to kids. Speak to the **parent**, in parent words. ([tikadtools kids products](https://tikadtools.com/blog/tiktok-ads-kids-products/))

**Our setup for the later paid test:** 18+, all genders, no interests at first (or one test ad group with "Baby & Kids Products"), English for US/UK, a separate German ad group (DE/AT/CH) if we ever make a German listing, Traffic objective to Amazon, and 3-5 creatives per ad group. Boost the best organic post as a Spark Ad.

## 6) Five TikTok concepts for Little Crayon Tales (no kids, no hands)

All are 9:16, with sound on (soft music or a calm voiceover), hook text in the first 1-2 seconds, and the Amazon cover + "Search 'Benny the Bear Cozy Day' on Amazon" at the end.

**Concept 1: "Satisfying color fill" (ASMR timelapse)** (based on GAME DISTRICT / MWM, the longest-running format)
- Format: a digital coloring timelapse of one Benny page (black-and-white to full color), with crayon scratch sounds (ASMR). The sentence from that page fades in at the end in big letters.
- Hook text (0-2 s): "Watch Benny's bedtime page come alive 🖍️"
- Length: 12-20 s, loopable (the last frame matches the first).
- Caption: "One big picture + one easy sentence on every page. Made for ages 3-6. 🐻 Benny the Bear's Cozy Day is on Amazon. #coloringbook #toddleractivities #bedtimeroutine #satisfying"

**Concept 2: "Color AND read" benefit flip-through** (based on PageEight, the top reach in the niche)
- Format: a digital flip-through of 5-6 pages. Each page shows for 1.5 s, and the sentence is read aloud by an AI or self-recorded calm voice while the big words highlight word by word.
- Hook text: "Your 3-year-old can't read yet? Start here."
- Length: 15-25 s.
- Caption: "Every page = 1 big picture to color + 1 short sentence in BIG letters. Kids color, you read together, and they start to know the words. #learntoread #preschoolactivities #kidsbooks #colorandread"

**Concept 3: "Cozy bedtime routine" voiceover slideshow** (based on Macmillan "Get cosy this autumn" and THINK ABOUT bedtime)
- Format: a slideshow of colored Benny pages in story order (cocoa, bath, pajamas, bed), with a warm voiceover reading the sentences and a lo-fi lullaby. Warm, cosy color filter.
- Hook text: "POV: bedtime finally feels cozy 🐻🌙"
- Length: 20-30 s.
- Caption: "A cozy everyday story your kid colors themselves. 10 minutes of calm before sleep. Ages 3-6. #bedtimeroutine #cozyseason #toddlermom #kidsbooks"

**Concept 4: "Christmas countdown with Nico" photo carousel** (based on gcreativegroup #christmaspresents, Usborne's September push, and the carousel engagement data)
- Format: a TikTok photo carousel of 6-8 slides. Slide 1 is a big hook text over a colored Nico page. Slides 2-6 are "uncolored vs colored" pairs. The last slide is the Amazon cover + "Stocking stuffer under $10" (use our real price).
- Hook text (slide 1): "The stocking stuffer kids actually finish 🦌🎄"
- Length: 7 slides, swipeable, with trending soft Christmas music.
- Caption: "Nico the Little Reindeer's Snowy Day: big, easy pictures + one short sentence per page. Perfect for 3-6 year olds on Christmas morning. #stockingstuffers #christmasactivities #kidschristmas #coloringbook"
- Timing: post from mid-October. Boost in November and early December.

**Concept 5: "Which one is your kid?" theme picker (series teaser)** (based on the theme hooks: princess/unicorn apps, monster truck, fairy Malbuch)
- Format: a split-screen or 4-panel slideshow: Benny (cozy) / Nico (Christmas) / Rocco (monster truck, "coming soon") / Posy (unicorn, "coming soon"). Each panel colors itself in a fast timelapse. The text asks parents to comment.
- Hook text: "Bear, reindeer, monster truck or unicorn? Which one is your kid? 👇"
- Length: 10-15 s.
- Caption: "Little Crayon Tales: color & read story books for ages 3-6. Tell us which one to finish next! #kidsbooks #monstertruck #unicorn #toddleractivities"
- Why: it drives comments (the algorithm boost), builds the series (outside data says series make the money), and tests demand for Rocco and Posy before launch.

**Paid test plan (later):** run each concept organically. After 3-5 days, boost the 1-2 posts with the best watch time / saves as Spark Ads (Traffic to Amazon, 18+, all genders, about $10-20/day for 5-7 days, 3-5 creatives per ad group). Keep the winner running "always on" at a low budget, like the 583-day fairy Malbuch ad.

## 7) What to avoid

- **Book title as the only hook** (I Can Color Too!: 16 variants and dead in 2 days).
- **Hashtag-only captions** with no hook (pw.books, jusy758: 1 day).
- **"Order now!" with no reason** (Editions Bidaya: 5 days).
- **Talking to kids** or a "childish" ad style: this breaks TikTok policy for under-13 products in the EU/UK/US. Always speak to the parent.
- **Targeting under 18**, or narrowing too early (women only, many interests). Successful ads stay broad.
- **Polished TV-style ads and slow intros.** TikTok says the hook must land in 3 seconds and recommends a "not overly polished" style.
- **Too much text at once**: max 5-10 words per second, and one text block per shot.
- **Personalisation promises** we cannot deliver (we can borrow the "your child" words, but do not say "personalised").
- **Starting Christmas too late**: Usborne and gcreativegroup started in late September or early October.

## 8) Confidence and gaps

**Confidence: medium-low for "what wins", high for "the niche is nearly empty".**

- **Small sample of real kids' book ads.** The library search is keyword- and relevance-based and returns many off-topic ads (shoes, cruises, "Kinder" chocolate). Only about 15-20 of about 700 ads were real kids' book or activity book ads. Most had 0-1K reach, so "winners" are judged mostly by **longevity**, not reach.
- **The EU hides age/gender splits** ("-"). Only UK ads show age and gender targeting, which I read for 4 ads (Bolly's, Macmillan, Trendflow, gcreativegroup).
- **No video lengths.** Videos do not load in a hidden tab, so I could not read durations. The length advice comes from the TikTok guide and app ads.
- **Ad captions only, not the full video script.** Hooks inside the video (text on screen, voiceover) could not be read. Some ads had empty captions (Hachette, Usborne, Albatros, Carlsen).
- **Detail pages opened for only 13 ads.** I ran out of time before opening Usborne, THINK ABOUT, GAME DISTRICT, MWM and Tweezorg.
- **EU only.** This library does not show US-only ads, and the US is our main Amazon market. See report 03 (TikTok Creative Center Top Ads) for US data.
- The previous window (Oct 2024 to Oct 2025) showed only 3 "coloring book" ads (Wild Life Inc., MILK Tailor Made Books). I did not study it further.
- Some searches returned 0 results (Ausmalbuch, kolorowanki/kolorowanka). Polish and Czech kids' coloring ads are almost absent.
- Spark Ad, carousel and CPC numbers come from agency blogs and TikTok's own marketing, which may be optimistic. Use them only as direction.
