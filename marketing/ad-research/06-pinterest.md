# 06 - Pinterest (Ads Repository + Trends + Search) - Little Crayon Tales

Research date: 2026-10-04. Researcher: AI agent (read-only, nothing was posted, saved or changed).
Sources: Pinterest search results (data from Pinterest's own search API, about 1,150 pins across 22 searches), Pinterest autocomplete and "guided search" chips, Pinterest Ads Repository (tried, see gaps), Pinterest Trends (tried, see gaps), plus web articles on Pinterest best practices.

> Important: the browser was in the Czech Republic (cz.pinterest.com), so search results are a bit personalised and show **almost no ads** (ads are mostly shown to US users). The patterns still hold, but treat the numbers as "good direction", not exact truth.

---

## 1) TL;DR (5 lines)

1. **Post Nico (Christmas) pins NOW.** Pinterest users plan Christmas from Aug-Oct and pins need 45-60+ days to "warm up". From now until about 10 Dec, roughly 2 of every 3 pins should be Nico / Christmas / stocking stuffer.
2. **Short coloring videos are the No. 1 format for KDP books linking to Amazon.** 38% of all top pins were videos. Almost every KDP seller who links to Amazon in these results uses a 6-15 sec "coloring one page" video.
3. **Title = page subject + "Coloring Page/Book" + "for Kids" + age.** Example: "Cute Reindeer Christmas Coloring Page for Kids 3-6". Pinterest search is keyword-based. Hashtags hardly matter.
4. **Fresh pins win.** 39% of top-ranked pins were less than 30 days old and 56% less than 90 days. Make a new image or video every day (new page, new layout). Don't repin the same picture.
5. **Use our unique angle in the text overlay:** "Color + Read in one book", "Big easy pages + 1 simple sentence", "Screen-free quiet time". No competitor in the results uses the "color & read / first reader" angle on Pinterest, so that space is empty.

- The page did not load in the automated (hidden) browser tab. It is a heavy app, and its data API needs a logged-in session, so it returned nothing.

**Patterns:**
1. **Tall pins dominate (69% are 2:3 or taller).** Square pins were rare (about 1-6 per search). Never post the square 8.5x8.5 page alone. Put it on a tall 1000x1500 canvas.
2. **Video share is highest on "activity" and "gift" searches** (toddler activities 51%, screen free 58%, gift ideas 45%). Parents searching for activities and gifts like to see "it in action".
3. **Fresh content is pushed hard.** Age of the top pins: under 30 days 39%, 30-90 days 18%, 90-365 days 30%, 1-2 years 9%, over 2 years 5%.
4. **Who ranks:** "Uploaded by user" pins with no link (13-26 per search), **payhip.com** (digital printables, 4-7 per search), free-printable blogs (mondaymandala, directcoloring, coloringbug), **etsy.com**, Google Drive/Docs freebies, and **amazon.com / amzn.to** (2-6 per search on bear, reindeer, Christmas and stocking stuffer searches).
5. **Carousels are almost absent** (1 in 791). Carousel is not a winning organic format in this niche right now.
6. **Highest engagement** (reactions) goes to DIY/activity videos (a "DIY little trinket" gift video: 52k reactions; toddler activity videos: 1k-5k). For coloring, the best were free-printable pages (Patrick Star 820, Spider-Man 679, "Kawaii Ice Cream Coloring Page for Kids - FREE Printable" video 443, "FREE Printable Coloring Page for Kids!" video 434). **"Free printable" is the strongest hook on Pinterest coloring searches. It is the top guided-search chip on almost every coloring query.**

## 6) Pin design rules

- **Size:** 1000 x 1500 px (2:3). Video: 1080 x 1920 (9:16) or 1000 x 1500. Never square-only.
- **Show the real page:** the black-and-white line art should take up 50-70% of the pin. People search coloring pages to *see* pages.
- **Text overlay:** max 6-8 words, big bold rounded font, readable on a phone (top or bottom band). Put the **age** on the pin ("Ages 3-6").
- **Hook ideas:** "Color + Read in One Book", "Big Easy Pages for Little Hands", "Screen-Free Quiet Time", "Cute Christmas Coloring for Ages 3-6".
- **Colors:** cream or white background, 1-2 soft brand colors (warm red/green for Nico, honey/brown for Benny, orange/blue for Rocco, pink/lilac for Posy). Partly colored pages pop more than all-white.
- **Branding:** small "Little Crayon Tales" logo at the bottom corner. Show the book cover small (corner or last frame of the video).
- **Video:** first 1-2 seconds = text hook + crayon already moving. No long intro. 7-15 s. Last frame = cover + "Find it on Amazon" (text only, no fake buttons).
- **Title:** keyword first, max ~100 characters. E.g. "Cute Reindeer Christmas Coloring Page for Kids 3-6 | Color & Read Book".
- **Description:** 1-2 natural sentences with 2-3 keywords + "Ages 3-6, 8.5x8.5 paperback, big bold lines, one easy sentence per page." No keyword lists.
- **Board:** each pin goes to the most specific keyword board first.
- **Freshness:** every pin should be a NEW image/video (new page, new crop, new hook). Same link is OK.
- **Frequency:** 1 pin a day is fine. Quality beats quantity (2026 guides say 3 strong pins a week beat 20 random ones).

## 8) What to avoid

- **Trademarks/brands in titles or tags:** Bluey, Hot Wheels, Monster Jam, El Toro Loco, Rudolph, Elf on the Shelf, Adopt Me, Coco Wyo, Bobbie Goods, Disney. They show up as search chips, but using them can get pins removed and confuses buyers.
- **"Free printable" in the title** when the link goes to a paid Amazon book (bad clicks, unhappy users).
- **Square-only page images** and tiny text that you can't read on a phone.
- **Same image pinned again and again.** Pinterest favours fresh pins (56% of top pins were under 90 days old).
- **Starting Christmas too late.** Pins posted in December often never catch the wave.
- **Keyword stuffing** in descriptions and lots of hashtags. Write normal sentences.
- **Adult "cozy coloring" look.** Many cozy pins are for adults (Coco Wyo style). Always add "for kids" and an age so the right parents click.
- **Fake buttons** ("Buy now" drawn on the image) or misleading claims. Keep it honest.
- **Carousel-heavy plans.** This format almost never shows up in the niche right now.
