# 07 – Amazon search ads on kids' coloring book searches

Research date: **Oct 4, 2026**. Source: amazon.com search pages (Books department), the Best Sellers list, 10 product pages, our 2 live listings, plus web guides on Amazon Ads for KDP.
Method: read-only browser (not signed in). **Important:** Amazon saw the browser as being in the **Czech Republic** ("Deliver to Czech Republic"). US shoppers may see slightly different ads. See section 8.

---

## 1) TL;DR (5 lines)

1. **Fix the listing before you pay for ads.** Both our books show **$12.00** on Amazon (the plan says $8.99), have **0 reviews, no A+ content and no series**, and Benny's author name looks misspelled ("Chobot, Františkek"). Clicks we pay for will not turn into sales like this.
2. **Each big search has about 9 paid spots on page 1** (3 at the top, 3 in the middle, 3 at the bottom) plus 2 paid carousels. Big series (Silly Bear, FairyWren, Little Cat Press) with 800–22,000 reviews own the big searches ("coloring book for kids/toddlers").
3. **The small searches are wide open:** "reindeer coloring book", "bear coloring book kids", "color and read book" and "early reader coloring book" have only 3–5 ads, and the best organic books there have **1–14 reviews**. This is where our $5/day can win.
4. **Shelf winners all use the same title formula:** *[Theme] Coloring Book for [Kids/Toddlers] Ages X-Y: 50 Big & Easy [things] (Series name)*, priced **$5.75–$9.99**, with **50–110 pages**, often "single-sided". Our titles start with a character name nobody searches for.
5. **Ads plan:** start mid/late October (Nico first), ~$5/day per book, **bids $0.25–0.45**, auto + one manual campaign with long-tail keywords and **competitor book pages (ASINs)**. Stay away from head terms and from "stocking stuffers for kids 3-5" (toys own it). Turn off Nico around Dec 18–20.

## 2) Sponsored ads per search (page 1, Books department)

**How the page is built (the same on every big search):**
- **Sponsored Products (SP):** 3 at the top (positions 1–3), 3 in the middle (13–15), 3 at the bottom (31–33). Small searches show only 3–5.
- **Paid carousels** (labelled "Sponsored"): *"Recently bought and rated | 4+ stars and rising in past 90 days"*, *"Seen on social media"*, *"Picks from Amazon Influencers"*, *"More Activity Books for Children"*.
- **Bottom ad slot**: an empty "Sponsored" box at the very bottom (a footer display/brand slot). It did not load for us.
- **Sponsored Brands headline banner or video:** **not seen on any search** (maybe because of the Czech location or the hidden tab; see section 8). Note: KDP only allows Sponsored Brands once one pen name has **3 or more books** in Author Central. We will have 4 after Rocco and Posy.
- No "Editorial recommendations" block on these searches.

Legend: Rev = number of ratings. BS = "Best Seller" badge. Price is what Amazon showed.

### Gap list + fixes (most important first)
1. **Price $12.00 vs plan $8.99.** On a shelf where 100-page books cost $5.75–$9.99, $12 for 30 pages looks expensive. *Fix:* in KDP, check the US list price and set **$8.99** (or $7.99 for Nico in Q4: an "under $10" impulse buy). If KDP already says $8.99, check again from a US address. Our location might change what is shown. At $8.99 you keep about $3.09 per sale (60% minus ~$2.30 print; check the KDP calculator).
2. **Author name typo on Benny ("Františkek").** If the author fields differ, Amazon may create **two different authors**. That breaks the Author Page and the "3 books under one pen name" rule for Sponsored Brands. *Fix:* open Benny in KDP and compare the author first/last name with Nico, character by character. Published paperbacks often lock the author name. If so, ask KDP Support to fix it, then claim both books in **Author Central** (author.amazon.com) with a photo and short bio.
3. **0 reviews.** Every ad winner has social proof. *Fix:* add a short "If your little one enjoyed this book, a review helps a lot" line on the last page of the next books (asking is allowed; paying or swapping is not). Share the book with real buyers via social media. **Do not** ask family or friends for reviews: Amazon removes them and can punish the account.
4. **No A+ content.** For paperbacks Amazon only shows 2 images, so **A+ is the only way to show the inside pages and the story.** Winners use it as a sample gallery plus a **series cross-sell table**. *Fix (KDP > Marketing > A+ Content, free):* module 1, a banner "Color the story. Read the story." with 3 sample spreads (picture + big sentence); module 2, "How it works for ages 3-6" with 3 icons (color, read, cuddle up); module 3, a comparison table of all Little Crayon Tales books (Benny, Nico, Rocco, Posy) with their themes and ages. One A+ can go on all books.
5. **No series.** Winners show "(Silly Bear Coloring Books)" or "Book 1 of 12", and a series page gives one-click cross-sells. *Fix:* KDP > Series > create **"Little Crayon Tales: Color & Read Story Books"** and add all books. The series name then shows up in search titles: free brand plus keywords.
6. **Title doesn't contain the search phrase "coloring book".** Shoppers type "bear coloring book" or "reindeer coloring book", not "Benny". *Fix:* a published paperback's title/subtitle is usually locked (changing it may need a new edition and new ISBN, and reviews don't carry over). So: for Benny/Nico, put the phrases into the **7 keyword boxes** and the A+ text. For **Rocco and Posy, use the winner formula before publishing:**
   - *Rocco the Monster Truck's Big Race: A Monster Truck Coloring Book for Kids Ages 3-6 | Color & Read Story with Big Easy Words | Bold and Easy Pages for Toddlers & Preschool (Little Crayon Tales)*
   - *Posy the Unicorn's Magic Day: A Unicorn Coloring Book for Girls Ages 3-6 | Color & Read Story with Big Easy Words | Cute Bold and Easy Pages (Little Crayon Tales)*
   - Formula: **[Character story title]: A [Theme] Coloring Book for [Kids/Girls/Toddlers] Ages 3-6 | Color & Read Story with Big Easy Words | [Bold and Easy / Stocking Stuffer] (Series)**. Keep it under ~200 characters and readable. No brand names.
7. **"Printed on both sides".** Winners brag about "single-sided pages, no bleed-through". *Fix for the next books:* black & white paperbacks with 24–108 pages cost the **same flat print fee** (please check this in the KDP calculator). So Rocco/Posy could be **single-sided** (about 60 pages) at no extra cost. Then the description can say "single-sided pages, no bleed-through, 50+ pages". That fixes the "too few pages" problem too.
8. **Page count looks small.** 27–29 pictures vs "50+ designs". *Fix:* lead with what makes us different. "One sweet story kids color AND read" is the value, not the page count. In the description and A+, say "27 story pages + a This Book Belongs To page". Single-sided printing (point 7) raises the page number.
9. **Reading age not shown.** Theo & Cleo shows "Reading age: 3 - 5 years". *Fix:* check in KDP that Reading age 3–6 is saved (it can take a few days to show).
10. **Categories / BSR.** None yet (too new). After the first sale, check that the book sits in *Children's Coloring Books* and *Bears* / *Christmas* categories. If not, request categories via KDP Support ("Contact us > Amazon product page > Update categories"), up to 3.

## 5) Amazon Ads plan for us

### When
- **Not before:** price fixed, author name checked, A+ live, series created. These are all free and take 1–3 days.
- **Nico:** start around **Oct 20–25** (Q4 needs time to learn; Christmas buying starts in November). Stop or lower it around **Dec 18–20** (print-on-demand delivery before Christmas), then pause.
- **Benny:** start at the same time (it fits all year). Keep it running in late December, when gift cards get spent.
- **Rocco / Posy:** 2 weeks after launch, once A+ and series are added.
- Ideally have 1–5 real reviews first. If none, still test with a small budget, but expect a high cost per sale.

### Budget (simple)
- **$5/day per book**, so $10/day for 2 books (about $300/month if fully spent; low bids usually spend less).
- Hard stop rules (same as the master plan): **a keyword or ASIN with 20+ clicks and 0 sales gets paused or added as a negative. A campaign with $30 spent and 0 sales gets paused.**
- Break-even: at $8.99 you keep about $3.09, so break-even ACOS is about **34%** (ACOS = ad spend ÷ sales). At $12 it would be ~41%, but fewer people buy. In launch weeks accept up to ~60–70% (you are buying data and first reviews), then aim for under 34%.
- Max useful bid: royalty × conversion rate. $3.09 × 10% gives **about $0.31 per click** to break even. So **keep bids around $0.25–0.45.** Books CPC averages about $0.38 and Q4 is about 30% more expensive (web sources below). Head terms will cost more than we can afford.

## 6) What the Amazon shelf tells us for our social ads

1. **Amazon has a paid shelf called "Seen on social media".** Books and sets that people talk about on TikTok/Instagram get an extra carousel on the search page. Social ads don't just bring clicks; they can unlock this shelf. Always send traffic to the exact amazon.com/dp/ link.
2. **Shelf words beat clever words.** Winners say the theme + "coloring book" + age in the first 5 words. Put the same in ad text and on the first frame of videos: **"A reindeer coloring book that's also a story. Ages 3-6."**
3. **The promise that converts is "big and easy for little hands".** "Big & Easy", "Bold & Easy", "Simple & Big", "for toddlers" are in nearly every winning title. Show a 3-year-old's crayon staying inside thick lines.
4. **Proof by number.** Winners shout "50+ designs", "100 pages". Our number is different: **"27 pages = 1 story your child colors and reads"**. Show the page flip: picture, then the big sentence, then the colored picture.
5. **Gift occasion in the hook.** "Stocking Stuffer" is literally in winning titles. For Nov–Dec use "Stocking stuffer under $10 that isn't more plastic" (already in our scripts). That only works if the price really is under $10. **Fix the $12 first.**
6. **Story is allowed on the coloring shelf.** Picture-book publishers (Patrick Picklebottom, The Day I Had a Dinosaur, Never Let a Unicorn…) pay to appear on coloring searches. "Coloring book + bedtime story in one" is a real, different hook. Lead with it.
7. **Don't sell the concept alone.** Theo & Cleo, Magic Forest and Vlad all sell "read and color" and are not selling. The hook must be **theme first** (reindeer, bear, monster truck, unicorn) and **color & read second**.
8. **Show what single-sided winners show:** clean thick lines, no bleed-through. For our double-sided books, show crayons and colored pencils (no markers) to avoid complaints.

## 7) What to avoid

- **Running ads while the price is $12.00, with no A+ and no reviews.** That's money for clicks that don't buy (see Theo & Cleo at #4M).
- **Bidding on head terms**: "coloring book for kids", "coloring book for toddlers", "unicorn coloring book for girls". 9 ads each from series with 1K–22K reviews, and top CPC in Q4.
- **"stocking stuffers for kids 3-5"**: 14 ads, all toys ($7 LCD tablets). Wrong shopper.
- **"bold and easy" / "cozy" without negatives**: these words bring adult and teen coloring shoppers (Coco Wyo "Cozy Spaces", "for Adults and Kids").
- **Trademark names** in titles, keywords or ads: Bluey, Rudolph, Disney, Ms. Rachel, Monster Jam, Hot Wheels.
- **Fake or friend reviews, review swaps, gift-for-review**: against Amazon rules.
- **Changing title/author on a live paperback without checking**: it can force a new edition and lose the page history.
- **Spreading $5/day over too many targets.** Start with the 30 above, cut losers at 20 clicks.
- **Raising bids in December on things that never sold.** Only raise proven winners.
- **Copying the "100 pages" game.** We can't win on volume. Win on "one story your child colors and reads", plus single-sided pages in the next books.
