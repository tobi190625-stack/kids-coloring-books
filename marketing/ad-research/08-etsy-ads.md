# 08 - Etsy ads and top Etsy listings (kids coloring pages and books)

Research date: Oct 4, 2026. Researcher: agent #8.

> **Important note on method (read first).** Etsy blocked us. Every Etsy page (search and /market pages) showed a
> DataDome CAPTCHA in the browser and gave "403 Forbidden" to direct fetches. We do not solve CAPTCHAs, and the
> Wayback Machine has no copies of Etsy search pages. So we could **not** read the "Ad by ..." labels inside Etsy.
> We used two other routes to Etsy data:
> 1. **Real paid Etsy ads on Google.** Etsy's "Offsite Ads" program pays Google to show sellers' listings. On normal
>    Google search they show up as **"Sponsored Products ... Etsy ... Visit Etsy"**. These are real, paid Etsy ads
>    (31 found). Our browser is in the Czech Republic, so these ads were EU-targeted and priced in EUR.
> 2. **Etsy listings in Google Shopping** (search "etsy + keyword"), about 300 listing rows over 13 searches. Google
>    gets these from Etsy's product feed. They show the title, price, sale badge and rating, so they work as a stand-in
>    for "top listings". We can't tell which of them were paid.
> 3. Keyword data (searches per month, number of listings, top shops) from RankHero, plus Etsy-seller guides.
>
> We could not look at the thumbnails because screenshots don't work in the hidden tab. Instead, we **measured the
> colours of 110 Etsy thumbnails with code**: how much is white, how much is coloured, and how much is red or green.
> The visual patterns in section 3 come from those numbers plus the titles and the seller guides.

---

## 1) TL;DR (5 lines)

1. **Etsy kids coloring is a cheap, crowded race.** Most printables cost **$1 to $4**, and almost half show a fake-looking **"35-70% OFF"**. The big hooks are a **big page count** ("100 / 210 / 10,000+ pages") plus the easy words **Easy / Bold & Easy / Toddler / Instant Download**.
2. **Personalized books are where the money and the paid ads are.** Custom-name coloring books sell for **$13-$37** with **hundreds to 3,300 reviews**. They got the highest ad bids we saw (CPC $1.91) and were the biggest group in Etsy's paid Google ads (personalized and physical books, EUR 13-65).
3. **Thumbnails that sell use colour.** Plain black-and-white line art looks empty when it's small. The best-reviewed listings show **part-coloured pages, crayons and a big number badge**. 84% of personalized-book thumbnails were full-colour lifestyle photos, and Christmas thumbnails were mostly **red**.
4. **Our "color and read" story idea is rare on Etsy** ("Coloring Storybook", "Read and Color", bedtime bear stories). There are only a few listings, with tiny review counts. That means little competition, but parents don't search those words. They search "**[theme] coloring pages for kids**", so we must use those words in pins and ads.
5. **Copy for Little Crayon Tales:** a split "B/W page → coloured by a kid" image with the **big sentence visible**, a count badge ("24 big pages + 1 story"), the age ("ages 3-6"), and seasonal red for Nico. The lead magnet is a **free printable 3-page mini story** with a "This story belongs to ____" name line. That gives parents the personalized feeling at zero cost.

## 3) Thumbnail / mockup patterns that dominate

**Measured colour data (110 thumbnails, scaled to 48x48 px):**

| Search | Thumbnails with clear colour (14%+ coloured pixels) | Pure black & white line art | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| kids coloring pages (34 unique) | **38%** | 47% | The best-reviewed ones (210 Easy, 145 Cartoon, Fall 4.9/87, 100,000+ bundle, Vehicles) are all in the colour group |
| christmas coloring pages (38) | **53%** | 32% | In most colour thumbnails, **red** was the main colour; a few were green |
| personalized coloring book (38) | **84%** | ~10% | 14 had less than 15% white, which means full photo scenes (kid, table, crayons, spiral book) |

**The 6 patterns (from the colour data, titles and seller guides):**

1. **"Pile of pages + big number" bundle collage.** Many pages fanned or in a grid, with a big badge such as "100 PAGES", "210", "10,000+". Usually a coloured banner on top. This is the most common style for bundles.
2. **Plain white single page.** Only black line art on white (for example RoseyJohnny and the "* Kids Printable Coloring Sheet *" sellers). It's cheap and fast to make, but at thumbnail size it looks empty and gets few reviews.
3. **Half-coloured page + crayons.** One page partly coloured in, with crayons or markers lying around it. This shows the parent the result. The seller guides recommend it: show 1-3 pages at an angle, a clean background, and a text overlay with the page count and format.
4. **Lifestyle photo (main style for personalized books).** A spiral-bound book with the child's name big on the cover, a crayon pack next to it, sometimes kid hands, on a wooden table or a pastel background. "**Free crayons**" is part of the offer.
5. **Seasonal colour frame.** Red and green borders, snow, Santa. Christmas thumbnails were clearly the reddest.
6. **Text overlay badges**, inferred from the words repeated in titles: "Instant Download", "PDF", "Bold & Easy", "Ages 3-5", "8.5x11", "+5 Bonus", "With Color Guide".

What is missing: hardly anyone shows **text on the page** (a story sentence). That makes our "big sentence on each page" easy to tell apart.

## 5) Price, bundle & offer patterns

- **Printables:** most cost $0.99-$4.00. RankHero medians: kids coloring pages $2.99, Christmas $2.99, unicorn $3.05, monster truck $3.00, dinosaur $2.84, bold & easy $4.99.
- **Fake discount everywhere:** about 40-45% of listings showed "XX% OFF" (25%, 35%, 50%, 59%, 69%, 70%). Etsy shoppers expect a "sale" badge.
- **Page-count arms race:** 15 / 30 / 50 / 100 / 150 / 200 / 210 / 1000 / 4000+ / 10,000+ / 100,000+ pages. "Price per page" ($0.03/ct) is even shown. You can't win on volume against free AI bundles. Win on story, quality and age fit instead.
- **Physical / personalized:** $12.94-$36.98 (photo books up to EUR 65). Extras that raise the price: spiral binding, "free crayons", wooden cover, name on cover, 6 or 30 theme choices.
- **Busy books:** printable $1-$18; felt physical $64-$88.
- **Learn-to-read printables:** $1.50-$9; the bundles were "was $18".
- **Bonus offers:** "+5 Bonus", "With Color Guide", PDF + PNG + JPG.

Meaning for us: on Amazon, a physical 8.5x8.5 book should be framed against the **$12-$20 "physical gift coloring book"** group (the paid-ad group), never against $2 PDFs.

## 7) What Little Crayon Tales should copy (5 visual concepts + lead magnet)

All formats: Pinterest 1000x1500 (2:3), Instagram/Facebook carousel 1080x1350 (4:5), and a square version for Amazon-style ads. Brand base: warm cream background (#FFF7E8), crayon red (#E63946), sunshine yellow (#FFC93C) and sky blue (#4EA8DE) as accents. Big rounded font (for example Baloo / Fredoka).

**Concept 1 - "Before → After" split (main ad)**
- Layout: one book page split down the middle. The left half is clean B/W line art, and the right half is the same page coloured with **real crayon texture**, a bit messy like a 4-year-old did it. The big story sentence ("Benny gets cozy in his blanket.") stays readable along the bottom.
- Text overlay (top): **"Color it. Then read it."** Small badge: "Ages 3-6".
- Why: it matches Etsy's half-coloured mockup pattern, adds the one thing Etsy lacks (the sentence), and uses colour so the thumbnail doesn't look empty.

**Concept 2 - "Fanned pages + count badge" (carousel cover / pin)**
- Layout: the cover top-left, with 4-5 interior pages fanned out at an angle on a cream background and 3-4 real crayons scattered around.
- Overlay: a round yellow badge with **"24 big pages + 1 cozy story"** (use the real page count), and under it "Big pictures · Big letters · Ages 3-6".
- Headline formula from the paid Etsy ads: **"Bear Coloring Book for Kids: [N] Big Simple Pages + a Bedtime Story"**.

**Concept 3 - "Lifestyle table shot" (gift angle, Q4)**
- Layout: the real 8.5x8.5 paperback open on a wooden table, small hands colouring, with a crayon box and a mug of cocoa (cozy). For Nico: fairy lights, pine branch, red mittens.
- Overlay: **"A cozy Christmas gift for ages 3-6"** + "Stocking stuffer" ribbon. Mention the price only if it is under $10-12.
- Why: 84% of personalized-book thumbnails (the expensive, best-reviewed group) are lifestyle photos, and their titles target "Grandma / Auntie gift".

**Concept 4 - "Red Christmas frame" (Nico seasonal pins, start now)**
- Layout: a red border with a white snowflake pattern. In the centre, Nico's best page, half-coloured (red nose, green scarf). Underneath, the sentence in big letters.

## 8) What to avoid

- **Volume wars** ("10,000+ / 100,000+ pages for $2"). We can't win there, and it makes a 24-page book look small. Say "1 story + 24 big pages" instead.
- **A black & white-only thumbnail.** It looks empty at small sizes. Always show colour (a coloured half, crayons, a seasonal frame).
- **Fake "70% OFF" badges** in our own pins and ads. They're normal on Etsy but cheap-looking, and Amazon/Meta ad rules don't allow fake discounts.
- **Keyword-stuffed text on images.** Comma-stacked titles work in Etsy's search box but not on a pin image. Keep it to 6 words or fewer on the image and put the keywords in the pin title and description.
- **Brand characters / memes** (Minecraft, Disney, Pokemon, Mario, Shrek, "brainrot"): copyright risk, and they don't fit a calm, cozy brand.
- **Mixing "kids & adults"** in one message. That's a common Etsy trick, but our buyer is a parent or grandparent of a 3-6 year old.
- **Unprovable claims** like "teaches reading in 7 days" or "stops bedtime fights". Say what the book *is* (big pictures, one sentence per page).
- Don't target the generic phrases "kids coloring pages" / "toddler coloring pages" (KD 93 / 78). Use theme + age phrases instead ("bear coloring book for kids 3-6", "monster truck coloring pages", "reindeer coloring page").
