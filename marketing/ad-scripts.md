# Short-form ad scripts (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)

All 15–30 s, vertical 9:16. Organic first; put money into paid ads only on the
videos that already perform organically. Link goes in bio (Amazon link or a
Linktree with all books).

## 1. "Watch it get colored" (best performer type)
- 0–2 s hook text: **"My 4-year-old colored this whole book in one day"** (only if true) or
  **"POV: you found a coloring book that also teaches reading"**
- 2–20 s: time-lapse of a page being colored with crayons/markers, story sentence visible.
- 20–25 s: flip through the rest of the book.
- End text: "Leo Learns to Roar – on Amazon, link in bio"

## 2. Before / after flip
- Hook: **"Blank page → masterpiece"**
- Show the black & white page, snap cut to the fully colored version. Repeat 4–5 pages to a beat.

## 3. Parent problem → solution
- Hook: **"Screen-free activity that actually keeps my kid busy"**
- Kid coloring at the table, parent voiceover: "Big pictures, one short sentence per page,
  so she's reading while she colors." Show the book cover. CTA.

## 4. Story read-aloud
- Read the whole 8-page story with the pages on screen (colored versions), cozy music.
- Works great on YouTube Shorts and as a Facebook Reel for parents/grandparents.

## 5. Gift angle (Nov–Dec, Easter, birthdays)
- Hook: **"Stocking stuffer under $10 that isn't more plastic"**
- Book next to a crayon pack and a bow. CTA.

## Rules that keep accounts safe
- Only show real kids with consent from their parent; you can show just hands.
- Don't claim results you didn't get ("sold 1000 copies" etc.).
- Say "#ad" / use the platform's paid-partnership label if someone else posts for you.
