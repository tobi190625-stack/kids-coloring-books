# Marketing plan: Benny the Bear's Cozy Day

Goal: send traffic to the Amazon listing. Amazon does the selling, printing and shipping.
Link everywhere: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HLVSLXK7 (live).

## 1. Pinterest (free, slow burn, lasts for months)
Images: `images/pin1-cover.png`, `pin2-pages.png`, `pin3-gift.png` (1000x1500).
Set each pin's destination link to the Amazon page. Post 1 pin a day for 30 days, re-using the 3 designs with new titles.
Pin titles (pick one per pin):
- Coloring book for kids ages 3-6: color the story and learn to read
- Screen-free activity for toddlers and preschoolers
- Easter basket stuffer idea: cute bear coloring story book
- Cozy bedtime story coloring book for kindergarten
Description template: "Benny the Bear's Cozy Day is a color and read story book for ages 3-6. Every page has one big picture to color and one short sentence in big letters. Find it on Amazon. #coloringbook #kidsactivities #screenfree #toddleractivities #preschool #kindergarten #giftsforkids"
Boards: Kids Activities, Screen-Free Fun, Easter Basket Ideas, Stocking Stuffers, Preschool Learning.

## 2. Meta (Facebook + Instagram)
Free: post `images/ad-square.png` and the pins to Facebook page + Instagram via Meta Business Suite (it posts to both at once).
Paid: start with ONE single-image ad using `ad-square.png`, objective "Traffic" to the Amazon link, $5-$10/day for 5 days, audience = parents/grandparents, ages 25-55, US, interests: coloring books, toddler activities, homeschooling. Turn off any ad that gets clicks but no sales after $30.
Ad text: "Screen-free fun kids actually finish. Color the story, learn to read. Ages 3-6. Now on Amazon."
Note: Amazon does not give sellers conversion data for outside traffic, so judge ads by whether sales rank and daily sales rise.

## 3. TikTok (organic)
Post 1-2 videos a day. Scripts (15-25 s, vertical):
1. "Watch a page get colored": timelapse of one page being colored, book cover at the end. Text: "A coloring book that teaches reading."
2. "Flip through": flip all pages fast to music, end on the cover.
3. "Screen-free that works": kid's hands coloring at a table, voiceover: "Big pictures, one short sentence per page, she is reading while she colors."
4. "Before / after": black-and-white page, then the same page colored.
5. "Gift idea": book with crayons and a bow. Hook: "Easter basket stuffer that isn't candy."
Only show real children with a parent's permission (hands only is fine). Link in bio = Amazon page.

## 4. TikTok Shop: honest answer
TikTok Shop is a storefront where the SELLER ships the orders. A KDP book is printed and shipped by Amazon, and Amazon only fulfills Amazon orders, so **you cannot list the KDP book in TikTok Shop and have Amazon ship it**. To sell there you would have to buy author copies at print cost, hold stock, and ship them yourself, and TikTok Shop's book category and eligibility rules vary by country and can change (check TikTok Seller Center; I could not verify current rules from here). The margin would be small after TikTok's fees, so I do not recommend it for this book.
Better use of TikTok: organic videos with the Amazon link in your bio, and later an Amazon-linked product tag only if TikTok offers it for your account.
Later idea: sell a printable PDF of the pages on Etsy or your own store, which you can deliver instantly.

## 5. Amazon itself (best paid channel for books)
After 2-3 weeks live: Amazon Ads > Sponsored Products, Auto campaign, $5/day, use the search terms from the keywords in listing.md. Pause anything with 20+ clicks and no sales.

## Weekly rhythm
Mon-Sun: 1 pin/day, 1 TikTok/day, 3 Instagram/Facebook posts. Review sales every Sunday. Ask family and friends for honest reviews once the book is live (never buy reviews).
