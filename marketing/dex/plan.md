# Marketing plan: Dex the Little Dinosaur's Big Day

Goal: send traffic to the Amazon listing. Amazon does the selling, printing and shipping.
Link everywhere: the Amazon product page (get it from KDP Bookshelf once the book is live).
This book sells all year (birthdays, party favors, gifts), with a bump at Christmas.

## 1. Pinterest
Images: `images/pin1-cover.png`, `pin2-pages.png`, `pin3-gift.png` (1000x1500). Destination link = Amazon page.
1 pin a day, re-using the 3 designs with new titles.
Pin titles:
- Dinosaur coloring book for kids ages 3-6: color the story and learn to read
- Birthday gift idea for a dinosaur-loving toddler
- Screen-free activity for preschoolers who love dinosaurs
- Dinosaur party favor idea: a coloring story book
- Cute dinosaur story book for kindergarten: color and read
Description: "Dex the Little Dinosaur's Big Day is a color and read story book for ages 3-6. Every page has one big picture to color and one short sentence in big letters. Find it on Amazon. #dinosaurs #coloringbook #kidsactivities #screenfree #toddleractivities #preschool #birthdaygifts #partyfavors"
Boards: Dinosaur Activities, Birthday Gifts for Kids, Screen-Free Fun, Party Favors, Preschool Learning.

## 2. Meta (Facebook + Instagram)
Free: post `images/ad-square.png` and the pins via Meta Business Suite.
Paid: ONE single-image ad with `ad-square.png`, objective "Traffic" to the Amazon link, $5-$10/day for 5 days,
audience parents/grandparents, ages 25-65, US, interests: dinosaurs, coloring books, toddler activities.
Turn off any ad with clicks but no sales after $30.
Ad text: "A dinosaur story kids color and read. Screen-free fun for ages 3-6. Now on Amazon."

## 3. TikTok (organic), 1-2 videos a day, 15-25 s, vertical
1. "Watch a page get colored": timelapse of the volcano or rainbow page, cover at the end.
2. "Flip through": flip all pages fast, end on the cover.
3. "Dino birthday gift": book, crayons and a bow. Hook: "Gift for the kid who loves dinosaurs."
4. "Before / after": black-and-white page, then the same page colored.
5. "Screen-free that works": hands coloring at a table, voiceover: "Big pictures, one short sentence per page, she is reading while she colors."
Only show real children with a parent's permission (hands only is fine). Link in bio = Amazon page.
TikTok Shop: skip (the seller must ship; KDP books can't be fulfilled that way).

## 4. Amazon Ads
After 2-3 weeks live: Sponsored Products, Auto campaign, $5/day, search terms from listing.md keywords.
Pause anything with 20+ clicks and no sales.

## Weekly rhythm
1 pin/day, 1 TikTok/day, 3 Instagram/Facebook posts. Review sales every Sunday.
Ask family and friends for honest reviews once live (never buy reviews).
