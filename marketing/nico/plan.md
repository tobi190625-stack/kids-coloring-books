# Marketing plan: Nico the Little Reindeer's Snowy Day

Goal: send traffic to the Amazon listing. Amazon does the selling, printing and shipping.
Link everywhere: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HLVTTK7T (live). For one link that works in every country, make a free Booklinker link from it.
Timing: this is a Christmas book. Start posting the day it goes live (October) and push hardest
until mid-December. Books ordered after about Dec 15 may not arrive in time, so post "last chance" pins then.

## 1. Pinterest
Images: `images/pin1-cover.png`, `pin2-pages.png`, `pin3-gift.png` (1000x1500). Destination link = Amazon page.
Post 1 pin a day, re-using the 3 designs with new titles.
Pin titles:
- Christmas coloring book for kids ages 3-6: color the story and learn to read
- Stocking stuffer idea for toddlers: cute reindeer coloring story book
- Screen-free winter activity for preschoolers
- Christmas gift for kindergarten kids that isn't more plastic
- Cozy winter story coloring book: Nico the little reindeer
Description: "Nico the Little Reindeer's Snowy Day is a color and read story book for ages 3-6. Every page has one big picture to color and one short sentence in big letters. Find it on Amazon. #christmasgifts #stockingstuffers #coloringbook #kidsactivities #screenfree #toddleractivities #preschool #winteractivities"
Boards: Christmas Gifts for Kids, Stocking Stuffers, Winter Activities, Screen-Free Fun, Preschool Learning.

## 2. Meta (Facebook + Instagram)
Free: post `images/ad-square.png` and the pins via Meta Business Suite (posts to both at once).
Paid: ONE single-image ad with `ad-square.png`, objective "Traffic" to the Amazon link, $5-$10/day for 5 days,
audience parents/grandparents, ages 25-65, US, interests: coloring books, Christmas gifts, toddler activities.
Turn off any ad with clicks but no sales after $30.
Ad text: "Stocking stuffer that isn't candy. Color the story, learn to read. Ages 3-6. Now on Amazon."
Note: Amazon gives no conversion data for outside traffic; judge by whether sales rank and daily sales rise.

## 3. TikTok (organic), 1-2 videos a day, 15-25 s, vertical
1. "Watch a page get colored": timelapse coloring the snowman page, cover at the end. Text: "A coloring book that teaches reading."
2. "Flip through": flip all pages fast to Christmas music, end on the cover.
3. "Stocking stuffer that isn't candy": book, crayons and a bow in a stocking.
4. "Before / after": black-and-white page, then the same page colored (snow angel or sled page).
5. "Screen-free that works": hands coloring at a table, voiceover: "Big pictures, one short sentence per page, she is reading while she colors."
Only show real children with a parent's permission (hands only is fine). Link in bio = Amazon page.
TikTok Shop: same answer as Benny. The seller must ship, KDP books can't be fulfilled that way. Skip it.

## 4. Amazon Ads
After 2-3 weeks live (so early November): Sponsored Products, Auto campaign, $5/day, search terms from listing.md keywords.
Pause anything with 20+ clicks and no sales. Turn ads off after December 20.

## Weekly rhythm
1 pin/day, 1 TikTok/day, 3 Instagram/Facebook posts. Review sales every Sunday.
Ask family and friends for honest reviews once live (never buy reviews).
