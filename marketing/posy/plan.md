# Marketing plan: Posy the Little Unicorn's Rainbow Birthday

Brand: Little Crayon Tales. Link everywhere: the book's Amazon page (from KDP Bookshelf once live). All text in English, posted at US times (14:00-16:00 or 00:00-02:00 Czech time).

## What Claude makes once the art is in (same as Benny and Nico)
- 3 Pinterest pins + 1 square ad: `python3 tools/make_marketing.py books/006-posy-the-unicorn` (needs a "marketing" block in book.json)
- 2 carousels x 6 slides: `python3 tools/make_carousel.py marketing/posy/carousel.json`
- Realistic coloring videos: color maps in `marketing/paint/posy-p<N>.json`, then `python3 tools/make_video.py books/006-posy-the-unicorn <page> "<hook>"` (add `--yt` for the YouTube "Link in bio" ending)
- Real colors for the paint maps: Posy white with a pastel rainbow mane and pink bow, Mochi cream/orange tabby, Bibi purple and pink, sky light blue, grass green, rainbow in true rainbow order

## Video hooks
- She brought the rainbow back
- Watch Posy come to life
- Birthday gift for a unicorn lover
- Which color does your kid pick first?

## Pin titles
- Unicorn coloring book for girls ages 3-6: color the story and learn to read
- Birthday gift idea for a unicorn-loving little girl
- Screen-free rainbow activity for preschoolers
- Unicorn party favor idea: a coloring story book

## Caption (Instagram / Facebook / TikTok)
```
It’s Posy’s birthday, but the rainbow has lost its colors! With Mochi the kitten and Bibi the butterfly, Posy collects red, orange, yellow, green, blue and purple from new friends to bring the rainbow back for a magical tea party. Ages 3-6. Link in bio!
#unicorn #unicornparty #coloringbook #kidsactivities #screenfree #toddleractivities #girlmom #birthdaygifts
```

## Meta ad text
```
A unicorn story she colors AND reads. Bring the rainbow back, one page at a time! Ages 3-6. Now on Amazon.
```

## Rhythm
1 pin a day, 1 video a day (TikTok + Reels + Shorts), 1 carousel and 1 single image per week. Paid ads only after the first sales and reviews ($5-10/day, US/UK/CA/AU, ages 25-65 parents).
