# Marketing plan: Rocco the Little Monster Truck's Big Jump

Brand: Little Crayon Tales. Link everywhere: the book's Amazon page (from KDP Bookshelf once live). All text in English, posted at US times (14:00-16:00 or 00:00-02:00 Czech time).

## What Claude makes once the art is in (same as Benny and Nico)
- 3 Pinterest pins + 1 square ad: `python3 tools/make_marketing.py books/005-rocco-monster-truck` (needs a "marketing" block in book.json)
- 2 carousels x 6 slides: `python3 tools/make_carousel.py marketing/rocco/carousel.json`
- Realistic coloring videos: color maps in `marketing/paint/rocco-p<N>.json`, then `python3 tools/make_video.py books/005-rocco-monster-truck <page> "<hook>"` (add `--yt` for the YouTube "Link in bio" ending)
- Real colors for the paint maps: Rocco red with a blue stripe, Dot teal, Gus green, big trucks orange and purple, mud brown, sky light blue

## Video hooks
- Watch Rocco come to life
- Gift for the kid who loves monster trucks
- SPLAT! Color the muddy page
- Which truck is your kid's favorite?

## Pin titles
- Monster truck coloring book for kids ages 3-6: color the story and learn to read
- Birthday gift idea for a truck-loving little boy
- Screen-free activity for preschoolers who love monster trucks
- Monster truck party favor idea: a coloring story book

## Meta ad text
```
A monster truck story kids color AND read. Big trucks, big letters, big fun. Ages 3-6. Now on Amazon.
```

## Rhythm
1 pin a day, 1 video a day (TikTok + Reels + Shorts), 1 carousel and 1 single image per week. Paid ads only after the first sales and reviews ($5-10/day, US/UK/CA/AU, ages 25-65 parents).
