The story

It’s a snowy morning, and Nico can’t wait to play outside. He makes a snow angel, meets his new friend Tilly the penguin, zooms down a hill on a red sled, builds a snowman, slides across the frozen pond, and cozies up with Mama for hot cocoa by the fire.

A sweet gift for

  • Christmas
  • Stocking stuffers
  • Birthdays
  • Snowy days at home

What’s inside

  • 29 full-page winter pictures that tell one sweet story
  • Bold, thick lines that are easy to color
  • One short sentence in big, clear letters on every page
  • A “This book belongs to” page for gifting
  • Square 8.5 x 8.5 inch pages

Best with crayons and colored pencils. The pictures are printed on both sides of the paper.
